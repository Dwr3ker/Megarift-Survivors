# Difficulty: rules (roadmap step 34)

Three difficulties for **Normal runs** (every map, including the region maps): **Normal** (today's game), **Hard** and
**Nightmare**. They sit beside **Hyper** (the double-speed switch) and combine with it. Numbers marked ⚙ are first
guesses to tune in play.

## What changes

| | Normal | Hard | Nightmare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enemy HP (bosses too) | ×1 | ×1.5 ⚙ | ×2.2 ⚙ |
| Enemy damage | ×1 | ×1.35 ⚙ | ×1.7 ⚙ |
| Enemy speed | ×1 | ×1.08 ⚙ | ×1.15 ⚙ |
| Elites (tougher, red-tinted enemies) | from minute 20 (today) | from minute 10, at least 4% ⚙ | from minute 3, at least 8% ⚙ |
| Alphas | every 8-12 min (today) | 25% more often ⚙ | 50% more often ⚙ |
| Events (invasions, mini-bosses, thieves) | every 4-5 min (today) | 15% more often ⚙ | 30% more often ⚙ |
| Coins at the end | ×1 | ×1.5 ⚙ | ×2.25 ⚙ |
| Tickets | as today | +1 ticket per 10 minutes survived ⚙ | +1 ticket per 5 minutes survived ⚙ |
| Experience | ×1 | ×1.15 ⚙ | ×1.3 ⚙ |
| Egg drop chance | as today | ×1.25 ⚙ | ×1.5 ⚙ |

- The multipliers apply on top of everything else the run already does (time scaling, the adaptive difficulty setting,
  late-run scaling, Eternamax, modifiers). The adaptive difficulty setting still eases a run you are losing.
- Your own Pokémon, moves and items are unchanged: difficulty only changes the enemies and the rewards.
- Hyper keeps its own effects (twice as fast, +30% XP, ×1.5 coins) and stacks with the difficulty: Hard + Hyper gives
  ×1.5 × 1.5 = ×2.25 coins.

## Unlocking
- **Hard** is open from the start.
- **Nightmare** unlocks when you **reach minute 30 on Hard** (when Arceus arrives), with or without Hyper (with Hyper
  the run clock runs twice as fast, so that is 15 real minutes). Until then its button shows a lock and that line.

## Records
- Best time is kept separately for each difficulty, with and without Hyper (6 records): Normal, Normal Hyper, Hard,
  Hard Hyper, Nightmare, Nightmare Hyper. The current Normal and Hyper records are the Normal ones.
- Region maps keep their own best time per region as today; the region record and Arceus unlocks count on any
  difficulty.
- The Profile shows the six records; the run summary and the HUD show the difficulty badge.

## Where it applies
- Normal runs only, like Hyper. Daily, Boss Rush, Tower, Rift Time Attack, Type, Team, Co-op, Party and the challenges
  keep their own balance. A saved run keeps its difficulty.

## Achievements
Minutes are run-clock minutes (the timer at the top), so Hyper reaches them twice as fast.
- *Hard as nails*: reach minute 15 on Hard.
- *Unbreakable*: reach minute 30 on Hard (also unlocks Nightmare).
- *Sweet dreams*: reach minute 15 on Nightmare.
- *Nightmare slayer*: defeat Arceus on Nightmare.
- *Wide awake*: defeat Eternatus on Nightmare (title *Nightmare*).
