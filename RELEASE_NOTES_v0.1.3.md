# Megarift Survivors v0.1.3

Released 2026-11-16. The full release of everything since 0.1.2: install over 0.1.2 or any 0.1.3 test build (Android version code 27); your progress is kept.

## New
- Terastallization: pick each Pokémon's Tera type in the Pokédex details, fill the Tera Orb in a run, and Terastallize once per run: a crystal transformation that powers up moves of your Tera type and changes your type for 45 seconds.
- Tera bosses: from minute 10, some bosses Terastallize at half HP and change type.
- Necrozma's forms: fuse into Dusk Mane or Dawn Wings from minute 30, then Ultra Burst into Ultra Necrozma from minute 45.
- Eggs: bosses, Alphas and rescues can drop an egg. Put it in the Incubator (main menu) and it hatches after you walk 1 to 4 km in your runs, with great IVs and twice the shiny chance.
- Hard and Nightmare difficulties for Normal runs, with their own records, rewards and achievements.
- A new mode select: big mode cards in groups (LB / RB), a bigger preview and a difficulty row.
- A full-screen region select with your progress, best time and Arceus status for every region.
- The Wardrobe: trails, auras and HUD frames for your Pokémon, earned from achievements, medals and the gacha.
- Aurora nights: some nights the sky lights up for 50 seconds, with wild shiny odds ×8 and +25% experience.
- Erase a taught TM on the Upgrades screen (it asks first); the TM stays bought.
- Evolution choices show the stone or item each one uses (Eevee's Fire, Water, Thunder Stone...).
- Late-run challenge for strong builds (Normal runs, Adaptive difficulty on): when you barely take damage, Pressure brings more, tougher enemies until you have to work again; it eases off as soon as you take real hits.
- Rift legendaries last longer for strong builds (about 20 to 27 s instead of a few seconds), while more power still means a shorter fight.

## Changes
- Legend Shards and eggs you pick up show on the run HUD beside coins and tickets.
- The run summary's New record line names the mode and difficulty and shows your old best; records are kept per mode and difficulty.
- Keys 4 to 9 pick cards when there are more than 3 (Eevee's evolutions).
- Redeem code moved to the trainer profile (the card under Medals), and the profile shows your Pokémon much bigger.
- The Weapon slots upgrade is gone: you always have 4 move slots, and the coins spent on it are refunded.
- A fusion that holds 4 moves can't be bonded any more; such bonds in a saved run are undone and the bond is given back.
- Controller power chords: X + LT Z-Move, X + RT Dynamax, Y + RT Tera. The D-pad only moves your Pokémon.
- Power buttons show the key or controller buttons that start them, and hints follow the input you are using.
- Z-Move, MAX and TERA can be moved and resized in the HUD layout editor; they no longer cover the stats panel.
- Gigantamax is +40% (Dynamax +30%), and the big Max wave stays with the G-Max move on top.

## Fixes
- Enemies and your co-op partner find their way around walls instead of getting stuck behind them; any that still get lost off-screen come back in.
- Mouse clicks no longer count as touches, which made some buttons (like Change Tera type) ignore clicks.
- The minimap's radar sweep is gone: in a still frame it looked like a stray line.
- The run summary's coin breakdown no longer shows "%d" when the run has extra notes (Hyper, eggs, Legend Shards).
- Hazards no longer cancel the protection you get while transforming, reviving or entering and leaving a rift.
- Closing the game while Arceus's arena is loading no longer crashes it.
- W, A, S and D move between the items in Kecleon's shop, like the arrow keys.

The feature PDF has new screenshots for Tera, Eggs, the difficulty and mode / region selects, the Wardrobe, the Aurora, the New record line, Erase TM and the evolution items.
