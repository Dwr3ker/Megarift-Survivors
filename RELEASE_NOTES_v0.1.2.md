# Megarift Survivors v0.1.2

Released 2026-11-01. Install over 0.1.1 (Android version code 13): your progress is kept.

## New
- **Achievements for the new features:** a **Regions** tab (Champion of Kanto … Champion of Paldea, Region hopper, World Champion), plus Perfect specimen / Flawless squad (31 in all three IVs), Nature's palette (all 11 natures on one Pokémon), Personal trainer (25 vitamins), Fresh breath (a Mint), Shard collector / Shard hoarder, Hand-picked legend / Legend curator, All in (a Max pull), Golden day / Gold standard (daily Gold medals), Week of medals (a 7-day daily streak) and Layaway / Regular customer (Kecleon reservations).

## Changes
- **Regions unlock by defeating Arceus** (the Judgment at minute 30) on the previous region, not just by surviving 30 minutes. Regions already unlocked in 0.1.1 stay open; players who have beaten Arceus before start with Johto open, and Eternatus winners with every region. The region picker shows "Arceus defeated" on cleared maps.
