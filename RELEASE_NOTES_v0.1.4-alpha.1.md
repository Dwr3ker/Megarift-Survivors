# Megarift Survivors v0.1.4-alpha.1 (test build)

Released 2026-11-21 as a pre-release for testing. Install over 0.1.3 (Android version code 28): your progress is kept.

## Changes
- A redesigned move bar: square tiles with one big icon, an x4 badge on a fusion, the bonded move in the corner, a cooldown sweep only on slow moves, every move's level pips under its tile, and the selected slot's full name above the bar.
- Power combinations (Z-Move, Dynamax, Tera) can be changed in Settings > Controls: hold the two buttons you want. Triggers work on controllers that send them as buttons.
- The right stick scrolls the list you are in, in every menu and on the pause screen.
- The Upgrades screen lights up the row you point at or are on, and the price button you hover is clearly brighter.
- The Upgrades screen shows your Pokémon much bigger, filling its frame.

## Fixes
- Arceus now has an HP bar in the Judgment.
- Tera, Z-Move and Dynamax combinations now work on controllers that send L2 / R2 as buttons, and a button that is half of a combination (like Select) no longer opens the Build summary while you hold it for the combination.
- Challenges #73 and #74 no longer stay on Coming soon: #73 is now "fill all 4 move slots and defeat 600", #74 Evolved Arsenal "evolve 4 moves and defeat 1,200".

## Downloads
- Android: `megarift-survivors-0.1.4-alpha.1.apk`
- Windows: `megarift-survivors-0.1.4-alpha.1-windows.zip` (unzip and run Megarift Survivors.exe)
