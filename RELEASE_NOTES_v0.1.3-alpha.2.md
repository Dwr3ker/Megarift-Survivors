# Megarift Survivors v0.1.3-alpha.2 (test build)

Released 2026-11-02 as a pre-release for testing. Install over 0.1.3-alpha.1 (Android version code 15): your progress is kept.

## Changes
- **Faint moves are easier to see:** Confusion is a bigger, bolder spiral with stars around it. Other thin move animations (Dream Eater, Dazzling Gleam, Agility, Heat Wave, Fairy Wind, Air Cutter, Thunder Wave, Dark Pulse, Fire Spin, Flame Wheel, Slash, Hypnosis, Psystrike, Psyshock, Psychic) have thicker strokes or a dark outline so they read on grass.
- **Sharper text:** the pixel font is drawn with hard pixel edges instead of soft grey ones.

## Fixes
- **Map items during fights:** Poké Ball Pouches, Elixirs and Prism Crystals are picked up, and mystery parcels and supply crates show the Open button, during boss and legendary fights too. They used to freeze for the whole fight. Only new items stop appearing during a fight.
- An item that is no use right now (bag full, a power-up already active, no enemies nearby) says why it stays on the ground.
- **Legendary shrine wall:** after summoning a legendary, the shrine used to turn into a small statue while its walls stayed, leaving an invisible wall with only the front open. The used shrine now stays visible, so its walls and stairs are clear.

## Downloads
- Android: `megarift-survivors-0.1.3-alpha.2.apk`
- Windows: `megarift-survivors-0.1.3-alpha.2-windows.zip` (unzip and run Megarift Survivors.exe)
