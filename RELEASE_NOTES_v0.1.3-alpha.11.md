# Megarift Survivors v0.1.3-alpha.11 (test build)

Released 2026-11-06 as a pre-release for testing. Install over 0.1.3-alpha.10 or earlier (Android version code 24): your progress is kept.

## Changes
- Enemy attack warnings are clear danger zones on any floor, and your allies' attacks look different: a dashed outline in their colour with no danger fill.
- A summoned legendary and caught allies each keep their own spot around you instead of walking on top of each other.
- The chip with your buffs and altar power sits beside the stats panel, so it no longer covers the MAX, Z-Move and Dash buttons.

The feature PDF has new screenshots for every screen that changed in 0.1.3.

## Downloads
- Android: `megarift-survivors-0.1.3-alpha.11.apk`
- Windows: `megarift-survivors-0.1.3-alpha.11-windows.zip` (unzip and run Megarift Survivors.exe)
