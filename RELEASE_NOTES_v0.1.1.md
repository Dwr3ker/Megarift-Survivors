# Megarift Survivors v0.1.1

Released 2026-11-01. Install over 0.1.0 (Android version code 12): your progress is kept.

## New
- **Region maps:** after Play on a Normal run, choose Kanto … Paldea or every region. Each region has its own two biomes and its own wild Pokémon, bosses, rift legendaries and roaming legendaries. Kanto is open; each next region unlocks by surviving 30 minutes on the previous one. Saves that already beat Eternatus start with every region open; saves whose best run reached 30 minutes start with Johto open.
- **IVs and natures:** every owned Pokémon has IVs (0-31 in HP, Attack and Speed, -5% to +5% each) and a nature (+10% / -5%, or neutral). Duplicates raise IVs and unlock their own nature (free to switch to). Vitamins (HP Up, Protein, Carbos: +3 IV for 300 coins) and Mints (a new nature for 1,000 coins) are on the Pokémon page.
- **Legend Shards and Legend Pick:** 1 shard per legendary duplicate and per Eternatus defeat; 5 shards pick any legendary you don't own.
- **Move previews:** a new move's level-up card shows a looping animation of how it attacks.

## Changes
- The Gacha page fits on one screen (pull buttons in two rows of three).
- The item screen's "Never offer again" is now "Not this run"; items blocked forever by the old button come back.

## Fixes
- Pickups are no longer missed: reach scales with the Pokémon's size, fast steps and dashes can't skip over them, and pulled pickups keep up with the player.
